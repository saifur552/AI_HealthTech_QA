**Bug Title:** System Accepts Past Date and Redirects to Summary Page

**Severity:** High

**Environment:** All environments

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Log in to the system.
2. Select a past date for scheduling an appointment.
3. Proceed to confirm the appointment.

**Expected Result:** The system should reject the scheduling of appointments for past dates and display an error message prompting the user to select a future date.

**Actual Result:** The system accepts the scheduling of appointments for past dates, redirects to the summary page, and allows further actions to be taken.
