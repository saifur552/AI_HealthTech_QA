**Bug Title:** System allows selection of past date and redirects to summary page

**Severity:** Major

**Environment:** Web Platform

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open the application and navigate to the appointment scheduling section.
2. Select a past date for the appointment.
3. Continue with the appointment booking process.

**Expected Result:** The system should not allow the selection of past dates for appointments and should show an error message or prevent proceeding to the summary page.

**Actual Result:** The system allows selection of past dates and redirects to the summary page.

**Note:** This issue impacts the integrity of the appointment scheduling feature and needs to be addressed promptly.
